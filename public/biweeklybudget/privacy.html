Biweekly Budget Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Biweekly Budget ("the app") is an Android budgeting app published by Todd Horst. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or share your personal information. Your budgets and transactions stay on your device.

Data you enter

Budget names, amounts, and transactions you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer has no access to them, and the app has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Backups

The app supports Android's built-in auto-backup. If you have Google backup turned on for your device, Android may save the app's data to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your device settings and Google's privacy policy, not by the developer. You can turn it off in your device's system settings.

Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time "Pro" purchase through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the Pro purchase; it does not receive or store your payment details, name or email.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed to work with Google Play Billing (internet access, network state, and the Play billing permission). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or other personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all data it holds on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue or message the developer at github.com/thorst.
